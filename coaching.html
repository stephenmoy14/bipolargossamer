Am I the coach for you?

Who I can help

I help people who…

  • struggle with mental health and want advice on what skills to practice
  • struggle with routine, motivation, and personal discipline and would like productivity tips
  • struggle with bipolar disorder and are looking for coping skills from a peer with lived experience
  • suspect they have bipolar disorder and are looking for peer insight before seeking a professional opinion
  • want to improve their overall mental health and don’t know where to start

I can support people with skills for:

  • Bipolar disorder
  • Depression disorders
  • Anxiety disorders
  • General mental health
  • Productivity challenges

Consider other support if…

  • You are currently in crisis. Please seek healthy in-person supports or text or call 988.
  • You are experiencing severe psychosis or mania. Please reach out to trusted family and friends, and seek professional help for immediate support. I’m not equipped to support current severe episodes, but I can help with skills to avoid mania in the future.
  • You are seeking clinical treatment or medications.
  • You are diagnosed with a condition I’m unfamiliar with. I’m not equipped to support:
    • PTSD (post-traumatic stress disorder)
    • BPD (borderline personality disorder)
    • Personality disorders
    • Schizophrenia
    • OCD (obsessive-compulsive disorder)
    • Other specific disorders

I do not have specialized training for all mental health conditions. I provide coaching with knowledge gained from my psychology degree, personal experiences, and skills I’ve learned through mental health programs I’ve attended.
